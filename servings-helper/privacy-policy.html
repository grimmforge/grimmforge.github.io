Servings Helper Privacy Policy

Developer: Grimm Forge · Effective date: 28 September 2026

Servings Helper (package com.grimmforge.servingshelper) does not collect, transmit or share any personal or usage data.

  • The names and weights of your cookware, the weights you enter and your settings (theme, weight unit, language, sorting) are stored only on your device.
  • The app does not connect to the internet, has no user accounts, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.
  • The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything it has stored.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
